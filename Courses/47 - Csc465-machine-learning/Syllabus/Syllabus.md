# Course Syllabus: CSC465-ML — Machine Learning

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y4S2 (Year 4, Semester 8)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Machine Learning Paradigms: Supervised, Unsupervised, Reinforcement
- **Week 2**: Linear Regression, Gradient Descent & Regularization (Ridge, Lasso)
- **Week 3**: Logistic Regression & Binary/Multiclass Classification
- **Week 4**: Support Vector Machines (SVM) & Kernel Trick
- **Week 5**: Ensemble Learning: Random Forests, Boosting (AdaBoost, XGBoost)
- **Week 6**: Neural Networks Fundamentals: Perceptron, Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLP)

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
