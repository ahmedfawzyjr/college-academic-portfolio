# Course Syllabus: CS213 — Design & Analysis of Algorithms

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y2S2 (Year 2, Semester 4)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Asymptotic Analysis & Recurrence Relations (Master Theorem)
- **Week 2**: Divide and Conquer (Merge Sort, Quick Sort, Binary Search)
- **Week 3**: Greedy Algorithms (Huffman Coding, Kruskal, Prim)
- **Week 4**: Dynamic Programming (0/1 Knapsack, LCS, Shortest Path)
- **Week 5**: Graph Algorithms (BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford)
- **Week 6**: NP-Completeness & P vs NP Introduction

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
