# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS213 — Design & Analysis of Algorithms

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Asymptotic Analysis & Recurrence Relations (Master Theorem) and Divide and Conquer (Merge Sort, Quick Sort, Binary Search).
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Route Optimization & Graph Algorithm Visualizer Engine.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Design & Analysis of Algorithms to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
