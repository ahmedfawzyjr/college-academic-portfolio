# Course Objectives: CS213 — Design & Analysis of Algorithms

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in design & analysis of algorithms.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Asymptotic Analysis & Recurrence Relations (Master Theorem) and Divide and Conquer (Merge Sort, Quick Sort, Binary Search).
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using C++17, Python, Benchmarking Tools.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across NP-Completeness & P vs NP Introduction.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
