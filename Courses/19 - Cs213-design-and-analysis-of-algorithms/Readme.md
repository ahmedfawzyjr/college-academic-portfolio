# CS213: Design & Analysis of Algorithms (تحليل وتصميم الخوارزميات)

**Course Code:** `CS213`  
**Academic Term:** Y2S2 (Year 2, Semester 4)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Core CS  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Design & Analysis of Algorithms is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Asymptotic Analysis & Recurrence Relations (Master Theorem), Divide and Conquer (Merge Sort, Quick Sort, Binary Search), Greedy Algorithms (Huffman Coding, Kruskal, Prim). The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Route Optimization & Graph Algorithm Visualizer Engine**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `CS211`, `CS201`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Asymptotic Analysis & Recurrence Relations (Master Theorem).
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing C++17, Python, Benchmarking Tools.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Route Optimization & Graph Algorithm Visualizer Engine**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Asymptotic Analysis & Recurrence Relations (Master Theorem)**
2. **Divide and Conquer (Merge Sort, Quick Sort, Binary Search)**
3. **Greedy Algorithms (Huffman Coding, Kruskal, Prim)**
4. **Dynamic Programming (0/1 Knapsack, LCS, Shortest Path)**
5. **Graph Algorithms (BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford)**
6. **NP-Completeness & P vs NP Introduction**
