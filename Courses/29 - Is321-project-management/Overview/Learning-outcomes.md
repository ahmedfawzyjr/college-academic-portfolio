# Measurable Learning Outcomes: IS321 — Project Management

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Project Life Cycle & Stakeholder Management and Scope Management & Work Breakdown Structure (WBS).
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Software Engineering Project Management Plan & WBS Suite.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Project Management to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
