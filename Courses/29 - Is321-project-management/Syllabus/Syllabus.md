# Course Syllabus: IS321 — Project Management

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y3S1 (Year 3, Semester 5)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Project Life Cycle & Stakeholder Management
- **Week 2**: Scope Management & Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
- **Week 3**: Time Management & Critical Path Method (CPM/PERT)
- **Week 4**: Cost Estimation, Earned Value Management (EVM)
- **Week 5**: Risk Management & Mitigation Strategies
- **Week 6**: Agile & Scrum Methodologies

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
