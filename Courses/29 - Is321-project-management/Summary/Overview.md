# Executive Overview: IS321 - Project Management

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Project Management (إدارة المشروعات)
- **Code:** `IS321`
- **Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 3rd Year - Term 1

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Project Management.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
