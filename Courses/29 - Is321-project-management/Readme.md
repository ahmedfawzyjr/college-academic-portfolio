# IS321: Project Management (إدارة المشروعات)

**Course Code:** `IS321`  
**Academic Term:** Y3S1 (Year 3, Semester 5)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Business  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Project Management is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Project Life Cycle & Stakeholder Management, Scope Management & Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), Time Management & Critical Path Method (CPM/PERT). The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Software Engineering Project Management Plan & WBS Suite**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `IS101`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Project Life Cycle & Stakeholder Management.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Jira, MS Project, Trello, Markdown.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Software Engineering Project Management Plan & WBS Suite**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Project Life Cycle & Stakeholder Management**
2. **Scope Management & Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)**
3. **Time Management & Critical Path Method (CPM/PERT)**
4. **Cost Estimation, Earned Value Management (EVM)**
5. **Risk Management & Mitigation Strategies**
6. **Agile & Scrum Methodologies**
