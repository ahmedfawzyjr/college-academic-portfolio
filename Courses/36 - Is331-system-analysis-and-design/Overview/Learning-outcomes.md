# Measurable Learning Outcomes: IS331 — System Analysis & Design

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of SDLC Methodologies (Waterfall, Agile, Spiral) and Requirements Determination & Use Case Modeling.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Enterprise Academic Management System Analysis & UML Blueprint.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect System Analysis & Design to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
