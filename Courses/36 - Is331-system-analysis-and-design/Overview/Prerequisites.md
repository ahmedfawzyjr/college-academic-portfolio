# Prerequisites & Academic Chain: IS331 — System Analysis & Design

## Strict Prerequisites
- **Formal Requirements**: `IS101`, `IS211`

## Foundational Knowledge Requirements
Before engaging with the topics in System Analysis & Design, students are expected to possess:
- Familiarity with core computational thinking and problem decomposition.
- Solid grounding in the prerequisite concepts: IS101, IS211.

## Downstream Course Chain
Successful completion of `IS331` serves as a prerequisite for subsequent advanced coursework in the curriculum:
- Enhances competency in upper-division systems and applications.
- Directly supports the Graduation Project (`CS499`).
