# Executive Overview: CS101 - Introduction to Computer

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Introduction to Computer (مقدمة في الحاسب الآلي)
- **Code:** `CS101`
- **Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 1st Year - Term 1

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Introduction to Computer.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
