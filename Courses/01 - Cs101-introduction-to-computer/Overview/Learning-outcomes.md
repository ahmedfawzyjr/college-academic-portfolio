# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS101 — Introduction to Computer

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Computer Hardware & Architecture Fundamentals and Number Systems & Binary Logic.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of System Information & Resource Inspector CLI.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Introduction to Computer to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
