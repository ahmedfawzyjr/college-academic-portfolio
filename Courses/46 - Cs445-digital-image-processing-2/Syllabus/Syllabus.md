# Course Syllabus: CS445 — Digital Image Processing 2

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y4S2 (Year 4, Semester 8)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Frequency Domain Filtering & 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)
- **Week 2**: Image Restoration & Noise Models (Gaussian, Salt-and-Pepper)
- **Week 3**: Image Segmentation: Thresholding (Otsu), Region Growing & Watershed
- **Week 4**: Feature Extraction (SIFT, ORB, HOG)
- **Week 5**: Object Detection & Pattern Recognition in Vision
- **Week 6**: Introduction to Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for Images

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
