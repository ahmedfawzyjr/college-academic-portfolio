# CS445: Digital Image Processing 2 (معالجة الصور الرقمية 2)

**Course Code:** `CS445`  
**Academic Term:** Y4S2 (Year 4, Semester 8)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** AI & Data  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Digital Image Processing 2 is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Frequency Domain Filtering & 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT), Image Restoration & Noise Models (Gaussian, Salt-and-Pepper), Image Segmentation: Thresholding (Otsu), Region Growing & Watershed. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Fourier Transform Filtering & Otsu Image Segmentation Suite**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `CS444`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Frequency Domain Filtering & 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT).
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Python, OpenCV, NumPy, Scikit-Image.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Fourier Transform Filtering & Otsu Image Segmentation Suite**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Frequency Domain Filtering & 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)**
2. **Image Restoration & Noise Models (Gaussian, Salt-and-Pepper)**
3. **Image Segmentation: Thresholding (Otsu), Region Growing & Watershed**
4. **Feature Extraction (SIFT, ORB, HOG)**
5. **Object Detection & Pattern Recognition in Vision**
6. **Introduction to Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for Images**
