# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS445 — Digital Image Processing 2

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Frequency Domain Filtering & 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) and Image Restoration & Noise Models (Gaussian, Salt-and-Pepper).
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Fourier Transform Filtering & Otsu Image Segmentation Suite.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Digital Image Processing 2 to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
