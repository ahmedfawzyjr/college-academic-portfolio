# Measurable Learning Outcomes: IS211 — Database Systems 1

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Database Concepts & DBMS Architecture and Entity-Relationship (ER) & Enhanced ER Modeling.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of University Academic Management Relational Database.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Database Systems 1 to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
