# IS211: Database Systems 1 (قواعد البيانات 1)

**Course Code:** `IS211`  
**Academic Term:** Y2S2 (Year 2, Semester 4)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Information Systems  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Database Systems 1 is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Database Concepts & DBMS Architecture, Entity-Relationship (ER) & Enhanced ER Modeling, Relational Model & Relational Algebra. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **University Academic Management Relational Database**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `CS101`, `IS101`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Database Concepts & DBMS Architecture.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL, DBeaver.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **University Academic Management Relational Database**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Database Concepts & DBMS Architecture**
2. **Entity-Relationship (ER) & Enhanced ER Modeling**
3. **Relational Model & Relational Algebra**
4. **SQL: DDL, DML, Joins, Subqueries & Views**
5. **Functional Dependencies & Database Normalization (1NF to BCNF)**
6. **Integrity Constraints & Indexing Fundamentals**
