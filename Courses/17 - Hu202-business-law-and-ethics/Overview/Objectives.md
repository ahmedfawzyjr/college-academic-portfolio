# Course Objectives: HU202 — Business Law & Ethics

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in business law & ethics.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Introduction to Legal Systems & Contract Law and Intellectual Property & Open Source Licenses (GPL, MIT, Apache).
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using License Auditing, Compliance Checklists, GDPR Toolkits.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Professional Software Engineering Ethics (ACM/IEEE Codes).
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
