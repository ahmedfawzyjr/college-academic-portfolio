# Course Syllabus: HU202 — Business Law & Ethics

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y2S1 (Year 2, Semester 3)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Introduction to Legal Systems & Contract Law
- **Week 2**: Intellectual Property & Open Source Licenses (GPL, MIT, Apache)
- **Week 3**: Cybercrime, Data Privacy & GDPR Regulations
- **Week 4**: Digital Signatures & Electronic Contracts
- **Week 5**: Professional Software Engineering Ethics (ACM/IEEE Codes)

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
