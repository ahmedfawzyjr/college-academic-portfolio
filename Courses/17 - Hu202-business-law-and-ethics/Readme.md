# HU202: Business Law & Ethics (تشريعات وحقوق إنسان)

**Course Code:** `HU202`  
**Academic Term:** Y2S1 (Year 2, Semester 3)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Humanities  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Business Law & Ethics is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Introduction to Legal Systems & Contract Law, Intellectual Property & Open Source Licenses (GPL, MIT, Apache), Cybercrime, Data Privacy & GDPR Regulations. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Software Licensing & Data Privacy Compliance Auditor**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** None (Direct Entry)
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Introduction to Legal Systems & Contract Law.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing License Auditing, Compliance Checklists, GDPR Toolkits.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Software Licensing & Data Privacy Compliance Auditor**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Introduction to Legal Systems & Contract Law**
2. **Intellectual Property & Open Source Licenses (GPL, MIT, Apache)**
3. **Cybercrime, Data Privacy & GDPR Regulations**
4. **Digital Signatures & Electronic Contracts**
5. **Professional Software Engineering Ethics (ACM/IEEE Codes)**
