# IS101: Information Systems Fundamentals (أساسيات نظم المعلومات)

**Course Code:** `IS101`  
**Academic Term:** Y1S2 (Year 1, Semester 2)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Information Systems  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Information Systems Fundamentals is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Role of Information Systems in Business, Hardware & Software Infrastructure, Data Management & Business Intelligence. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Enterprise Information Architecture Blueprint**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** None (Direct Entry)
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Role of Information Systems in Business.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing UML, BPMN, Entity Relationship Diagrams.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Enterprise Information Architecture Blueprint**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Role of Information Systems in Business**
2. **Hardware & Software Infrastructure**
3. **Data Management & Business Intelligence**
4. **E-Commerce & Digital Enterprise**
5. **Enterprise Systems (ERP, CRM, SCM)**
6. **Information Systems Security & Ethics**
