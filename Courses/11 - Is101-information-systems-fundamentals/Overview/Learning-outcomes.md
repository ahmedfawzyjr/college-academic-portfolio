# Measurable Learning Outcomes: IS101 — Information Systems Fundamentals

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Role of Information Systems in Business and Hardware & Software Infrastructure.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Enterprise Information Architecture Blueprint.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Information Systems Fundamentals to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
