# Course Objectives: IS101 — Information Systems Fundamentals

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in information systems fundamentals.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Role of Information Systems in Business and Hardware & Software Infrastructure.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using UML, BPMN, Entity Relationship Diagrams.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Information Systems Security & Ethics.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
