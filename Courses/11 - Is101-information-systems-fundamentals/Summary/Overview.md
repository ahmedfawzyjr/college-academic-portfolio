# Executive Overview: IS101 - Information Systems Fundamentals

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Information Systems Fundamentals (أساسيات نظم المعلومات)
- **Code:** `IS101`
- **Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 1st Year - Term 2

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Information Systems Fundamentals.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
