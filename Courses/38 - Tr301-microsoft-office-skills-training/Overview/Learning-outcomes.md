# Measurable Learning Outcomes: TR301 — Microsoft Office Skills Training

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Advanced Excel (VLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, Pivot Tables, Macros) and Data Visualization & Dashboard Design.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Automated Financial & Academic Reporting Dashboard.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Microsoft Office Skills Training to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
