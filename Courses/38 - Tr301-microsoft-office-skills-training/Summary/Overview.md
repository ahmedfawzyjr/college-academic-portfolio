# Executive Overview: TR301 - Microsoft Office Skills Training

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Microsoft Office Skills Training (تدريب مهارات مكتبية)
- **Code:** `TR301`
- **Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 3rd Year - Term 2

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Microsoft Office Skills Training.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
