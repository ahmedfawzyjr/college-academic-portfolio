# Measurable Learning Outcomes: MA301 — Operations Research

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Mathematical Modeling & Optimization Problems and Linear Programming: Graphical Method.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Linear Programming Simplex Solver & Optimization Toolkit.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Operations Research to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
