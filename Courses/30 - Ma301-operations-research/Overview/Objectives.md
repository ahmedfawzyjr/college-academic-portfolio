# Course Objectives: MA301 — Operations Research

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in operations research.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Mathematical Modeling & Optimization Problems and Linear Programming: Graphical Method.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Python, PuLP, SciPy Optimize.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Network Models (Shortest Route, Minimal Spanning Tree).
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
