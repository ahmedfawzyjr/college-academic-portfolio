# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS331 — Computer Networks 1

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of OSI 7-Layer & TCP/IP 4-Layer Models and Application Layer: HTTP, DNS, SMTP, FTP.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Concurrent Multi-Client Server Messaging System.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Computer Networks 1 to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
