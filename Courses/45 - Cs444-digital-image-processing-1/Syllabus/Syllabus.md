# Course Syllabus: CS444 — Digital Image Processing 1

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y4S2 (Year 4, Semester 8)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Digital Image Fundamentals & Human Visual Perception
- **Week 2**: Image Sampling, Quantization & Color Models (RGB, Grayscale, HSV)
- **Week 3**: Intensity Transformations & Histogram Equalization
- **Week 4**: Spatial Filtering: Smoothing (Gaussian, Box) & Sharpening (Laplacian, Sobel)
- **Week 5**: Edge Detection (Sobel, Prewitt, Canny)
- **Week 6**: Morphological Image Processing (Dilation, Erosion, Opening, Closing)

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
