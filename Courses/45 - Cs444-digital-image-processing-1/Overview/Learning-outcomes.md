# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS444 — Digital Image Processing 1

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Digital Image Fundamentals & Human Visual Perception and Image Sampling, Quantization & Color Models (RGB, Grayscale, HSV).
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Computer Vision Image Enhancement & Edge Detection Suite.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Digital Image Processing 1 to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
