# CS444: Digital Image Processing 1 (معالجة الصور الرقمية 1)

**Course Code:** `CS444`  
**Academic Term:** Y4S2 (Year 4, Semester 8)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** AI & Data  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Digital Image Processing 1 is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Digital Image Fundamentals & Human Visual Perception, Image Sampling, Quantization & Color Models (RGB, Grayscale, HSV), Intensity Transformations & Histogram Equalization. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Computer Vision Image Enhancement & Edge Detection Suite**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `CS341`, `MA202`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Digital Image Fundamentals & Human Visual Perception.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Python, OpenCV, NumPy, Matplotlib.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Computer Vision Image Enhancement & Edge Detection Suite**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Digital Image Fundamentals & Human Visual Perception**
2. **Image Sampling, Quantization & Color Models (RGB, Grayscale, HSV)**
3. **Intensity Transformations & Histogram Equalization**
4. **Spatial Filtering: Smoothing (Gaussian, Box) & Sharpening (Laplacian, Sobel)**
5. **Edge Detection (Sobel, Prewitt, Canny)**
6. **Morphological Image Processing (Dilation, Erosion, Opening, Closing)**
