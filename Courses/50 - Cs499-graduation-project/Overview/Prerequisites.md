# Prerequisites & Academic Chain: CS499 — Graduation Project

## Strict Prerequisites
- **Formal Requirements**: `CSC465`, `CS313`, `IS312`, `CS415`

## Foundational Knowledge Requirements
Before engaging with the topics in Graduation Project, students are expected to possess:
- Familiarity with core computational thinking and problem decomposition.
- Solid grounding in the prerequisite concepts: CSC465, CS313, IS312, CS415.

## Downstream Course Chain
Successful completion of `CS499` serves as a prerequisite for subsequent advanced coursework in the curriculum:
- Enhances competency in upper-division systems and applications.
- Directly supports the Graduation Project (`CS499`).
