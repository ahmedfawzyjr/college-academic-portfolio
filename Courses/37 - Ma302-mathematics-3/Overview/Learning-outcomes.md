# Measurable Learning Outcomes: MA302 — Mathematics 3

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of First-Order Differential Equations (Separable, Exact, Linear) and Second-Order Linear Homogeneous & Non-Homogeneous Equations.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Differential Equation Numerical Solver (Runge-Kutta & Euler).
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Mathematics 3 to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
