# MA302: Mathematics 3 (رياضيات 3 (معادلات تفاضلية))

**Course Code:** `MA302`  
**Academic Term:** Y3S2 (Year 3, Semester 6)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Basic Science  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Mathematics 3 is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in First-Order Differential Equations (Separable, Exact, Linear), Second-Order Linear Homogeneous & Non-Homogeneous Equations, Laplace Transforms & Applications to Differential Equations. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Differential Equation Numerical Solver (Runge-Kutta & Euler)**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `MA101`, `MA202`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of First-Order Differential Equations (Separable, Exact, Linear).
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Python, SciPy, SymPy, NumPy.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Differential Equation Numerical Solver (Runge-Kutta & Euler)**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **First-Order Differential Equations (Separable, Exact, Linear)**
2. **Second-Order Linear Homogeneous & Non-Homogeneous Equations**
3. **Laplace Transforms & Applications to Differential Equations**
4. **Fourier Series & Periodic Functions**
5. **Partial Differential Equations (Heat, Wave Equations)**
6. **Numerical Methods for Differential Equations (Euler, Runge-Kutta)**
