# Course Syllabus: MA302 — Mathematics 3

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y3S2 (Year 3, Semester 6)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: First-Order Differential Equations (Separable, Exact, Linear)
- **Week 2**: Second-Order Linear Homogeneous & Non-Homogeneous Equations
- **Week 3**: Laplace Transforms & Applications to Differential Equations
- **Week 4**: Fourier Series & Periodic Functions
- **Week 5**: Partial Differential Equations (Heat, Wave Equations)
- **Week 6**: Numerical Methods for Differential Equations (Euler, Runge-Kutta)

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
