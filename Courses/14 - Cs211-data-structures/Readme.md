# CS211: Data Structures (هياكل البيانات)

**Course Code:** `CS211`  
**Academic Term:** Y2S1 (Year 2, Semester 3)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Core CS  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Data Structures is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Abstract Data Types & Big-O Complexity, Singly & Doubly Linked Lists, Stacks & Queues (Array & Linked Implementations). The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **High-Performance Task Management & Priority Queue Engine**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `CS111`, `CS112`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Abstract Data Types & Big-O Complexity.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing C++17, Java, Google Test / Catch2.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **High-Performance Task Management & Priority Queue Engine**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Abstract Data Types & Big-O Complexity**
2. **Singly & Doubly Linked Lists**
3. **Stacks & Queues (Array & Linked Implementations)**
4. **Binary Trees & Binary Search Trees (BST)**
5. **Balanced Trees (AVL Trees)**
6. **Hash Tables & Collision Resolution Strategies**
