# Course Syllabus: ST101 — Probability & Statistics

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y1S2 (Year 1, Semester 2)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Descriptive Statistics & Data Visualization
- **Week 2**: Probability Concepts & Bayes' Theorem
- **Week 3**: Discrete Distributions (Binomial, Poisson, Geometric)
- **Week 4**: Continuous Distributions (Normal, Exponential)
- **Week 5**: Sampling Distributions & Central Limit Theorem
- **Week 6**: Hypothesis Testing & Confidence Intervals

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
