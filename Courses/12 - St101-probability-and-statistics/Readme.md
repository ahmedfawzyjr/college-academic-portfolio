# ST101: Probability & Statistics (احتمالات وإحصاء)

**Course Code:** `ST101`  
**Academic Term:** Y1S2 (Year 1, Semester 2)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Basic Science  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Probability & Statistics is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Descriptive Statistics & Data Visualization, Probability Concepts & Bayes' Theorem, Discrete Distributions (Binomial, Poisson, Geometric). The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Statistical Distribution & Hypothesis Testing Analyzer**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `MA101`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Descriptive Statistics & Data Visualization.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Python, SciPy, Statsmodels, Seaborn.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Statistical Distribution & Hypothesis Testing Analyzer**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Descriptive Statistics & Data Visualization**
2. **Probability Concepts & Bayes' Theorem**
3. **Discrete Distributions (Binomial, Poisson, Geometric)**
4. **Continuous Distributions (Normal, Exponential)**
5. **Sampling Distributions & Central Limit Theorem**
6. **Hypothesis Testing & Confidence Intervals**
