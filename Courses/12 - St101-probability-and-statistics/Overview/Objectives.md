# Course Objectives: ST101 — Probability & Statistics

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in probability & statistics.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Descriptive Statistics & Data Visualization and Probability Concepts & Bayes' Theorem.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Python, SciPy, Statsmodels.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Hypothesis Testing & Confidence Intervals.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
