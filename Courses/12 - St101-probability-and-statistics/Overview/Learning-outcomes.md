# Measurable Learning Outcomes: ST101 — Probability & Statistics

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Descriptive Statistics & Data Visualization and Probability Concepts & Bayes' Theorem.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Statistical Distribution & Hypothesis Testing Analyzer.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Probability & Statistics to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
