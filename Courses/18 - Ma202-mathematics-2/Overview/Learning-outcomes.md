# Measurable Learning Outcomes: MA202 — Mathematics 2

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Systems of Linear Equations & Gaussian Elimination and Matrix Algebra, Inverses & Determinants.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Linear Algebra Matrix Operations & Decomposition Library.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Mathematics 2 to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
