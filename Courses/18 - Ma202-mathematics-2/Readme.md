# MA202: Mathematics 2 (رياضيات 2 (جبر خطي))

**Course Code:** `MA202`  
**Academic Term:** Y2S1 (Year 2, Semester 3)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Basic Science  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Mathematics 2 is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Systems of Linear Equations & Gaussian Elimination, Matrix Algebra, Inverses & Determinants, Vector Spaces, Subspaces & Basis. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Linear Algebra Matrix Operations & Decomposition Library**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `MA101`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Systems of Linear Equations & Gaussian Elimination.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Python, NumPy, SciPy.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Linear Algebra Matrix Operations & Decomposition Library**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Systems of Linear Equations & Gaussian Elimination**
2. **Matrix Algebra, Inverses & Determinants**
3. **Vector Spaces, Subspaces & Basis**
4. **Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors & Diagonalization**
5. **Orthogonality & Gram-Schmidt Process**
6. **Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) & Applications in CS**
