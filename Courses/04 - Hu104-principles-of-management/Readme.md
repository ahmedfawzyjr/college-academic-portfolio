# HU104: Principles of Management (مبادئ الإدارة)

**Course Code:** `HU104`  
**Academic Term:** Y1S1 (Year 1, Semester 1)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Business  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Principles of Management is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Management Functions: Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling, Organizational Structure & Culture, Strategic Decision Making. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Engineering Team Organizational & Governance Model**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** None (Direct Entry)
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Management Functions: Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Trello, Agile Management, Gantt Charts.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Engineering Team Organizational & Governance Model**.

## 📚 Topics

2. **Organizational Structure & Culture**
3. **Strategic Decision Making**
4. **Motivation & Leadership Theories**
5. **Operations Management & Quality Control**
