# Executive Overview: HU104 - Principles of Management

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Principles of Management (مبادئ الإدارة)
- **Code:** `HU104`
- **Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 1st Year - Term 1

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Principles of Management.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
