# Course Objectives: HU104 — Principles of Management

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in principles of management.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Management Functions: Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling and Organizational Structure & Culture.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Trello, Agile Management, Gantt Charts.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Operations Management & Quality Control.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
