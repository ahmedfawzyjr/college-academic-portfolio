# Measurable Learning Outcomes: HU104 — Principles of Management

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Management Functions: Planning, Organizing, Leading, Controlling and Organizational Structure & Culture.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Engineering Team Organizational & Governance Model.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Principles of Management to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
