# Prerequisites & Academic Chain: HU104 — Principles of Management

## Strict Prerequisites
- **Formal Requirements**: None (Direct Entry)

## Foundational Knowledge Requirements
Before engaging with the topics in Principles of Management, students are expected to possess:
- Familiarity with core computational thinking and problem decomposition.
- Solid grounding in the prerequisite concepts: General mathematical and computer literacy.

## Downstream Course Chain
Successful completion of `HU104` serves as a prerequisite for subsequent advanced coursework in the curriculum:
- Enhances competency in upper-division systems and applications.
- Directly supports the Graduation Project (`CS499`).
