# Measurable Learning Outcomes: EL101 — Computer Electronics

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Semiconductor Physics & PN Junction Diodes and Diode Applications (Rectifiers, Clippers, Clampers).
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Analog Circuit & Logic Gate Analysis Simulator.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Computer Electronics to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
