# EL101: Computer Electronics (إلكترونيات حاسب)

**Course Code:** `EL101`  
**Academic Term:** Y1S2 (Year 1, Semester 2)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Systems  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Computer Electronics is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Semiconductor Physics & PN Junction Diodes, Diode Applications (Rectifiers, Clippers, Clampers), Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT). The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Analog Circuit & Logic Gate Analysis Simulator**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `PH101`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Semiconductor Physics & PN Junction Diodes.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing SPICE, Proteus, Python.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Analog Circuit & Logic Gate Analysis Simulator**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Semiconductor Physics & PN Junction Diodes**
2. **Diode Applications (Rectifiers, Clippers, Clampers)**
3. **Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT)**
4. **Field-Effect Transistors (FET & MOSFET)**
5. **Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps)**
6. **Digital Logic Gate Electronics**
