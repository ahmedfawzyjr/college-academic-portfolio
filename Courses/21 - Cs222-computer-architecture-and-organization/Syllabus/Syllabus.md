# Course Syllabus: CS222 — Computer Architecture & Organization

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y2S2 (Year 2, Semester 4)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Instruction Set Architecture (ISA) & MIPS Assembly
- **Week 2**: Processor Datapath & Control Units
- **Week 3**: Pipelining & Pipeline Hazards (Data, Control, Structural)
- **Week 4**: Memory Hierarchy & Cache Design (Direct-mapped, Set-associative)
- **Week 5**: Virtual Memory & Translation Lookaside Buffers (TLB)
- **Week 6**: Input/Output Systems, Buses & DMA

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
