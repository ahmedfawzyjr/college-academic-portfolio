# Executive Overview: CS222 - Computer Architecture & Organization

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Computer Architecture & Organization (تنظيم وعمارة الحاسب)
- **Code:** `CS222`
- **Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 2nd Year - Term 2

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Computer Architecture & Organization.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
