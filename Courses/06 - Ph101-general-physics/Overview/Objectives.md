# Course Objectives: PH101 — General Physics

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in general physics.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Electric Charges & Coulomb's Law and Electric Fields & Gauss's Law.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Python, SciPy, Circuit Simulators.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Electromagnetic Induction & Faraday's Law.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
