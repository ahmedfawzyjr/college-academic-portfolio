# Measurable Learning Outcomes: PH101 — General Physics

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Electric Charges & Coulomb's Law and Electric Fields & Gauss's Law.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Electric Field & DC Circuit Numerical Simulator.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect General Physics to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
