# PH101: General Physics (فيزياء عامة (كهربية ومغناطيسية))

**Course Code:** `PH101`  
**Academic Term:** Y1S1 (Year 1, Semester 1)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Basic Science  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
General Physics is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Electric Charges & Coulomb's Law, Electric Fields & Gauss's Law, Electric Potential & Capacitance. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Electric Field & DC Circuit Numerical Simulator**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** None (Direct Entry)
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Electric Charges & Coulomb's Law.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Python, SciPy, Circuit Simulators.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Electric Field & DC Circuit Numerical Simulator**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Electric Charges & Coulomb's Law**
2. **Electric Fields & Gauss's Law**
3. **Electric Potential & Capacitance**
4. **Current, Resistance & Direct Current Circuits**
5. **Magnetic Fields & Ampère's Law**
6. **Electromagnetic Induction & Faraday's Law**
