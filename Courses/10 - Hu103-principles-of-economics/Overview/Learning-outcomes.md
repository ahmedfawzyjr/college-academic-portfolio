# Measurable Learning Outcomes: HU103 — Principles of Economics

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Supply, Demand & Market Equilibrium and Elasticity of Demand & Supply.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Market Equilibrium & SaaS Pricing Optimization Engine.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Principles of Economics to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
