# Course Objectives: HU103 — Principles of Economics

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in principles of economics.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Supply, Demand & Market Equilibrium and Elasticity of Demand & Supply.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Technology Economics & SaaS Margins.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
