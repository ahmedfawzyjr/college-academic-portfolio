# CS311: Automata & Formal Languages (النظرية الآلية واللغات الصورية)

**Course Code:** `CS311`  
**Academic Term:** Y3S1 (Year 3, Semester 5)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Theoretical CS  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Automata & Formal Languages is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA) & NFA, Regular Expressions & Regular Languages, Pumping Lemma for Regular Languages. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Finite State Automata & Regular Expression Engine**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `CS201`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA) & NFA.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Python, JFLAP, Automata Simulators.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Finite State Automata & Regular Expression Engine**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA) & NFA**
2. **Regular Expressions & Regular Languages**
3. **Pumping Lemma for Regular Languages**
4. **Context-Free Grammars (CFG) & Pushdown Automata (PDA)**
5. **Turing Machines & Computability Theory**
6. **Halting Problem & Decidability**
