# Course Objectives: CS311 — Automata & Formal Languages

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in automata & formal languages.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA) & NFA and Regular Expressions & Regular Languages.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Python, JFLAP, Automata Simulators.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Halting Problem & Decidability.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
