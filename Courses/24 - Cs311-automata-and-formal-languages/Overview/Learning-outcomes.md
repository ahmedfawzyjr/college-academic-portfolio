# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS311 — Automata & Formal Languages

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA) & NFA and Regular Expressions & Regular Languages.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Finite State Automata & Regular Expression Engine.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Automata & Formal Languages to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
