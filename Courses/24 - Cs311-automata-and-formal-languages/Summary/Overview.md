# Executive Overview: CS311 - Automata & Formal Languages

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Automata & Formal Languages (النظرية الآلية واللغات الصورية)
- **Code:** `CS311`
- **Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 3rd Year - Term 1

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Automata & Formal Languages.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
