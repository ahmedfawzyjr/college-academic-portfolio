# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS201 — Discrete Structures

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Propositional & Predicate Logic and Sets, Functions, Sequences & Summations.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Propositional Logic Solver & Graph Relations Engine.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Discrete Structures to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
