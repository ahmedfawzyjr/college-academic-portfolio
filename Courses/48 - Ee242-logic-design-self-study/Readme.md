# EE242: Logic Design (Self Study) (تصميم المنطق (دراسة ذاتية))

**Course Code:** `EE242`  
**Academic Term:** Y4S2 (Year 4, Semester 8)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Systems  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Logic Design (Self Study) is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Advanced Boolean Algebra & Minimization Algorithms (Quine-McCluskey), Design of Complex Sequential Circuits & Registers, Analysis and Design of Asynchronous Sequential Networks. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Quine-McCluskey Boolean Minimizer & Digital Circuit Synthesizer**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `CS221`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Advanced Boolean Algebra & Minimization Algorithms (Quine-McCluskey).
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Verilog, Python Logic Synthesizer, Logisim.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Quine-McCluskey Boolean Minimizer & Digital Circuit Synthesizer**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Advanced Boolean Algebra & Minimization Algorithms (Quine-McCluskey)**
2. **Design of Complex Sequential Circuits & Registers**
3. **Analysis and Design of Asynchronous Sequential Networks**
4. **Programmable Logic Devices (PLDs, CPLDs, FPGAs)**
5. **Hardware Description Language (VHDL/Verilog) Synthesis**
6. **Hazards in Digital Circuits & Glitch Prevention**
