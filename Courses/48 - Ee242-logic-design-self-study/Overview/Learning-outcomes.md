# Measurable Learning Outcomes: EE242 — Logic Design (Self Study)

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Advanced Boolean Algebra & Minimization Algorithms (Quine-McCluskey) and Design of Complex Sequential Circuits & Registers.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Quine-McCluskey Boolean Minimizer & Digital Circuit Synthesizer.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Logic Design (Self Study) to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
