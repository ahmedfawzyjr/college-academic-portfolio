# Course Syllabus: EE242 — Logic Design (Self Study)

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y4S2 (Year 4, Semester 8)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Advanced Boolean Algebra & Minimization Algorithms (Quine-McCluskey)
- **Week 2**: Design of Complex Sequential Circuits & Registers
- **Week 3**: Analysis and Design of Asynchronous Sequential Networks
- **Week 4**: Programmable Logic Devices (PLDs, CPLDs, FPGAs)
- **Week 5**: Hardware Description Language (VHDL/Verilog) Synthesis
- **Week 6**: Hazards in Digital Circuits & Glitch Prevention

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
