# Course Syllabus: HU401 — Creative Thinking & Innovation

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y4S2 (Year 4, Semester 8)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Creative Thinking Principles & Cognitive Frameworks
- **Week 2**: Lateral Thinking & Divergent vs Convergent Thinking
- **Week 3**: Mind Mapping & Systematic Brainstorming (SCAMPER)
- **Week 4**: Design Thinking Methodology: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test
- **Week 5**: TRIZ (Theory of Inventive Problem Solving) Introduction
- **Week 6**: Innovation Management & Intellectual Property Strategy

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
