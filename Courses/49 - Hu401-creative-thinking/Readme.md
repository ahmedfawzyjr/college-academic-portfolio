# HU401: Creative Thinking & Innovation (التفكير الإبداعي والابتكار)

**Course Code:** `HU401`  
**Academic Term:** Y4S2 (Year 4, Semester 8)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Humanities  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Creative Thinking & Innovation is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Creative Thinking Principles & Cognitive Frameworks, Lateral Thinking & Divergent vs Convergent Thinking, Mind Mapping & Systematic Brainstorming (SCAMPER). The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Design Thinking Innovation Canvas & Product Solution Blueprint**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** None (Direct Entry)
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Creative Thinking Principles & Cognitive Frameworks.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Design Thinking, Brainstorming Frameworks, Markdown.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Design Thinking Innovation Canvas & Product Solution Blueprint**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Creative Thinking Principles & Cognitive Frameworks**
2. **Lateral Thinking & Divergent vs Convergent Thinking**
3. **Mind Mapping & Systematic Brainstorming (SCAMPER)**
4. **Design Thinking Methodology: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test**
5. **TRIZ (Theory of Inventive Problem Solving) Introduction**
6. **Innovation Management & Intellectual Property Strategy**
