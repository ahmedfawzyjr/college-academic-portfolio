# Executive Overview: HU401 - Creative Thinking & Innovation

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Creative Thinking & Innovation (التفكير الإبداعي والابتكار)
- **Code:** `HU401`

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Creative Thinking & Innovation.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
