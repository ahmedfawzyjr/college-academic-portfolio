# Measurable Learning Outcomes: HU401 — Creative Thinking & Innovation

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Creative Thinking Principles & Cognitive Frameworks and Lateral Thinking & Divergent vs Convergent Thinking.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Design Thinking Innovation Canvas & Product Solution Blueprint.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Creative Thinking & Innovation to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
