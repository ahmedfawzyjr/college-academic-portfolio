# Course Objectives: HU401 — Creative Thinking & Innovation

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in creative thinking & innovation.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Creative Thinking Principles & Cognitive Frameworks and Lateral Thinking & Divergent vs Convergent Thinking.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Design Thinking, Brainstorming Frameworks, Markdown.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Innovation Management & Intellectual Property Strategy.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
