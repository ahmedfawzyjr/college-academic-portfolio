# HU302: Principles of Marketing (مبادئ التسويق)

**Course Code:** `HU302`  
**Academic Term:** Y3S2 (Year 3, Semester 6)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Business  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Principles of Marketing is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Marketing Concepts & Customer Value, Market Research & Consumer Behavior, Segmentation, Targeting & Positioning (STP). The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Tech Startup Go-To-Market & Digital Acquisition Strategy**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `HU104`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Marketing Concepts & Customer Value.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Google Analytics, Marketing Funnels, Markdown.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Tech Startup Go-To-Market & Digital Acquisition Strategy**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Marketing Concepts & Customer Value**
2. **Market Research & Consumer Behavior**
3. **Segmentation, Targeting & Positioning (STP)**
4. **Marketing Mix: 4Ps (Product, Price, Place, Promotion)**
5. **Digital Marketing, SEO & Growth Strategies**
6. **Product Life Cycle & Tech Launch Management**
