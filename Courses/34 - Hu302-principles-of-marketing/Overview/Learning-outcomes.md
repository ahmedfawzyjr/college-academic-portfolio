# Measurable Learning Outcomes: HU302 — Principles of Marketing

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Marketing Concepts & Customer Value and Market Research & Consumer Behavior.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Tech Startup Go-To-Market & Digital Acquisition Strategy.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Principles of Marketing to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
