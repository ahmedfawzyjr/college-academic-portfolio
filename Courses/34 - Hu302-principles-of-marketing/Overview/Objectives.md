# Course Objectives: HU302 — Principles of Marketing

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in principles of marketing.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Marketing Concepts & Customer Value and Market Research & Consumer Behavior.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Google Analytics, Marketing Funnels, Markdown.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Product Life Cycle & Tech Launch Management.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
