# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CSC465 — Software Engineering

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Software Processes & Agile Methodologies and Requirements Engineering & IEEE 830 SRS Specification.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Production-Grade Academic Service Architecture & TDD Suite.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Software Engineering to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
