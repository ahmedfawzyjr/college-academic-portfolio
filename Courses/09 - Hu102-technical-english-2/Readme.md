# HU102: Technical English 2 (لغة إنجليزية 2)

**Course Code:** `HU102`  
**Academic Term:** Y1S2 (Year 1, Semester 2)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Humanities  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Technical English 2 is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Engineering Proposals & Whitepapers, Writing Technical Reports, Cross-Cultural Communication. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Software Engineering Whitepaper & Specification Review**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `HU101`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Engineering Proposals & Whitepapers.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Markdown, Pandoc, Grammarly.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Software Engineering Whitepaper & Specification Review**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Engineering Proposals & Whitepapers**
2. **Writing Technical Reports**
3. **Cross-Cultural Communication**
4. **Oral Technical Defense & Pitching**
5. **Peer Review & Academic Editing**
