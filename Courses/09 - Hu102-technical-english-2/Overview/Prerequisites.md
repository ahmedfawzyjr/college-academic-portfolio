# Prerequisites & Academic Chain: HU102 — Technical English 2

## Strict Prerequisites
- **Formal Requirements**: `HU101`

## Foundational Knowledge Requirements
Before engaging with the topics in Technical English 2, students are expected to possess:
- Familiarity with core computational thinking and problem decomposition.
- Solid grounding in the prerequisite concepts: HU101.

## Downstream Course Chain
Successful completion of `HU102` serves as a prerequisite for subsequent advanced coursework in the curriculum:
- Enhances competency in upper-division systems and applications.
- Directly supports the Graduation Project (`CS499`).
