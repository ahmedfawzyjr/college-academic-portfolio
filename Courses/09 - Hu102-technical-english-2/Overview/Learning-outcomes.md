# Measurable Learning Outcomes: HU102 — Technical English 2

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Engineering Proposals & Whitepapers and Writing Technical Reports.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Software Engineering Whitepaper & Specification Review.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Technical English 2 to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
