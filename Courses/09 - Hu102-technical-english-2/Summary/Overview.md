# Executive Overview: HU102 - Technical English II

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Technical English II (لغة إنجليزية 2)
- **Code:** `HU102`
- **Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 1st Year - Term 2

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Technical English II.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
