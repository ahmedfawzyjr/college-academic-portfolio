# HU101: Technical English 1 (لغة إنجليزية 1)

**Course Code:** `HU101`  
**Academic Term:** Y1S1 (Year 1, Semester 1)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Humanities  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Technical English 1 is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Technical Vocabulary in Computing, Reading Technical Specifications, Grammar & Sentence Structure for Engineering. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Technical Documentation & Specification Portfolio**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** None (Direct Entry)
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Technical Vocabulary in Computing.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Markdown, LaTeX, Technical Documentation.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Technical Documentation & Specification Portfolio**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Technical Vocabulary in Computing**
2. **Reading Technical Specifications**
3. **Grammar & Sentence Structure for Engineering**
4. **Writing Executive Summaries**
5. **Technical Presentations**
