# Executive Overview: HU101 - Technical English I

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Technical English I (لغة إنجليزية 1)
- **Code:** `HU101`
- **Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 1st Year - Term 1

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Technical English I.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
