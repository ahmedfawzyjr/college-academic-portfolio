# MA101: Mathematics 1 (رياضيات 1 (تفاضل وتكامل))

**Course Code:** `MA101`  
**Academic Term:** Y1S1 (Year 1, Semester 1)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Basic Science  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Mathematics 1 is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Functions, Limits & Continuity, Differential Calculus & Derivatives, Applications of Derivatives (Optimization, Curve Sketching). The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Numerical Differentiation & Integration Engine**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** None (Direct Entry)
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Functions, Limits & Continuity.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Python, SymPy, NumPy, Matplotlib.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Numerical Differentiation & Integration Engine**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Functions, Limits & Continuity**
2. **Differential Calculus & Derivatives**
3. **Applications of Derivatives (Optimization, Curve Sketching)**
4. **Integral Calculus & Definite Integrals**
5. **Techniques of Integration**
6. **Series & Sequences**
