# Course Objectives: MA101 — Mathematics 1

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in mathematics 1.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Functions, Limits & Continuity and Differential Calculus & Derivatives.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Python, SymPy, NumPy.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Series & Sequences.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
