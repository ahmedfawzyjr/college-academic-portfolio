# Measurable Learning Outcomes: MA101 — Mathematics 1

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Functions, Limits & Continuity and Differential Calculus & Derivatives.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Numerical Differentiation & Integration Engine.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Mathematics 1 to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
