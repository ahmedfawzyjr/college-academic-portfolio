# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS321 — Operating Systems

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of OS Structures, System Calls & Interrupts and Processes, Threads & Multithreading Models.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Process Scheduling & Page Replacement Simulator.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Operating Systems to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
