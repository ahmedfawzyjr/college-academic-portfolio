# Course Syllabus: HU301 — Technical Report Writing

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y3S2 (Year 3, Semester 6)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Technical Communication Fundamentals
- **Week 2**: Engineering Report Structure (Abstract, Introduction, Methodology, Results)
- **Week 3**: Data Presentation: Charts, Diagrams, Tables
- **Week 4**: IEEE / APA Citation & Academic Integrity
- **Week 5**: Peer Review & Editing Technical Manuscripts

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
