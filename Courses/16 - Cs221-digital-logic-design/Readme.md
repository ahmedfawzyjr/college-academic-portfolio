# CS221: Digital Logic Design (تصميم المنطق الرقمي)

**Course Code:** `CS221`  
**Academic Term:** Y2S1 (Year 2, Semester 3)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Systems  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Digital Logic Design is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Boolean Algebra & Logic Simplification (K-Maps), Combinational Logic Design (Adders, Multiplexers, Decoders), Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) Architecture. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **4-Bit Arithmetic Logic Unit & FSM Digital Simulator**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** `EL101`
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Boolean Algebra & Logic Simplification (K-Maps).
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Verilog, Logisim, Digital Simulator.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **4-Bit Arithmetic Logic Unit & FSM Digital Simulator**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Boolean Algebra & Logic Simplification (K-Maps)**
2. **Combinational Logic Design (Adders, Multiplexers, Decoders)**
3. **Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) Architecture**
4. **Sequential Logic (Latches & Flip-Flops: SR, D, JK, T)**
5. **Registers, Counters & Shift Registers**
6. **Synchronous Finite State Machines (FSM)**
