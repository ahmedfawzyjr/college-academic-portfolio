# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS221 — Digital Logic Design

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Boolean Algebra & Logic Simplification (K-Maps) and Combinational Logic Design (Adders, Multiplexers, Decoders).
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of 4-Bit Arithmetic Logic Unit & FSM Digital Simulator.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Digital Logic Design to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
