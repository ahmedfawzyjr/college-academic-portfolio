# Executive Overview: CS221 - Digital Logic Design

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Digital Logic Design (تصميم المنطق الرقمي)
- **Code:** `CS221`
- **Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 2nd Year - Term 1

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Digital Logic Design.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
