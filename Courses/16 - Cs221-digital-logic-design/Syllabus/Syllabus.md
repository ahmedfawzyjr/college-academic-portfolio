# Course Syllabus: CS221 — Digital Logic Design

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y2S1 (Year 2, Semester 3)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Boolean Algebra & Logic Simplification (K-Maps)
- **Week 2**: Combinational Logic Design (Adders, Multiplexers, Decoders)
- **Week 3**: Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) Architecture
- **Week 4**: Sequential Logic (Latches & Flip-Flops: SR, D, JK, T)
- **Week 5**: Registers, Counters & Shift Registers
- **Week 6**: Synchronous Finite State Machines (FSM)

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
