# Course Syllabus: CSC466 — Data Mining

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y4S1 (Year 4, Semester 7)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Data Mining Process & CRISP-DM Methodology
- **Week 2**: Data Preprocessing: Cleaning, Integration, Transformation, Reduction
- **Week 3**: Association Rule Mining & Apriori Algorithm
- **Week 4**: Classification Algorithms (Decision Trees, Naive Bayes, k-NN)
- **Week 5**: Clustering Algorithms (K-Means, Hierarchical Clustering, DBSCAN)
- **Week 6**: Model Evaluation: Confusion Matrix, ROC-AUC, Cross-Validation

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
