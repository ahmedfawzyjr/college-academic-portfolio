# Course Syllabus: IS312 — Database Systems 2

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y3S2 (Year 3, Semester 6)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Transaction Processing & ACID Properties
- **Week 2**: Concurrency Control (Locking, Timestamping, 2PL)
- **Week 3**: Database Recovery Techniques (WAL, Checkpoints)
- **Week 4**: Query Processing & Optimization (Cost Estimation)
- **Week 5**: Stored Procedures, Triggers & PL/SQL
- **Week 6**: NoSQL Databases & Distributed Data Systems

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
