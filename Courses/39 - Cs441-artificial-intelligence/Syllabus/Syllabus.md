# Course Syllabus: CS441 — Artificial Intelligence

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y4S1 (Year 4, Semester 7)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Intelligent Agents & Environment Types
- **Week 2**: Uninformed Search (BFS, DFS, Uniform Cost Search)
- **Week 3**: Informed / Heuristic Search (A*, Greedy Best-First)
- **Week 4**: Adversarial Search & Game Playing (Minimax, Alpha-Beta Pruning)
- **Week 5**: Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSP)
- **Week 6**: Knowledge Representation & First-Order Logic Inference

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
