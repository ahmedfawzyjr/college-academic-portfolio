# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS441 — Artificial Intelligence

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Intelligent Agents & Environment Types and Uninformed Search (BFS, DFS, Uniform Cost Search).
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of A* Pathfinding & Minimax Adversarial Game Engine.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Artificial Intelligence to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
