# Prerequisites & Academic Chain: CS441 — Artificial Intelligence

## Strict Prerequisites
- **Formal Requirements**: `CS213`, `CS201`

## Foundational Knowledge Requirements
Before engaging with the topics in Artificial Intelligence, students are expected to possess:
- Familiarity with core computational thinking and problem decomposition.
- Solid grounding in the prerequisite concepts: CS213, CS201.

## Downstream Course Chain
Successful completion of `CS441` serves as a prerequisite for subsequent advanced coursework in the curriculum:
- Enhances competency in upper-division systems and applications.
- Directly supports the Graduation Project (`CS499`).
