# Course Objectives: HU201 — Principles of Accounting

## Primary Academic Objectives
The overarching educational goal of this course is to provide foundational and rigorous applied instruction in principles of accounting.

### Specific Objectives
1. **Theoretical Grounding**: Understand fundamental paradigms and mathematical/logical formulations in Accounting Equation & Double-Entry Bookkeeping and Journal Entries, General Ledger & Trial Balance.
2. **Applied Implementation**: Develop verified practical solutions using Python, SQL, Excel / Sheets.
3. **Engineering Analysis**: Analyze complexity, design trade-offs, and failure modes across Internal Controls & Financial Ratios.
4. **Professional Competency**: Apply industry-standard documentation, testing, and clean code practices.
