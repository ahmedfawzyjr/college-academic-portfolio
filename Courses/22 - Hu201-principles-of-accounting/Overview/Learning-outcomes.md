# Measurable Learning Outcomes: HU201 — Principles of Accounting

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Accounting Equation & Double-Entry Bookkeeping and Journal Entries, General Ledger & Trial Balance.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Double-Entry Ledger & Financial Statement Generator.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Principles of Accounting to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
