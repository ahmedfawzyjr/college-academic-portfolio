# HU201: Principles of Accounting (مبادئ المحاسبة)

**Course Code:** `HU201`  
**Academic Term:** Y2S2 (Year 2, Semester 4)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Curriculum Category:** Business  
**Instruction Type:** Compulsory  
**Department:** Department of Computer Science, Future Academy  

---

## 📌 Overview
Principles of Accounting is an integral component of the computer science curriculum, providing in-depth theoretical foundations and practical application in Accounting Equation & Double-Entry Bookkeeping, Journal Entries, General Ledger & Trial Balance, Financial Statements: Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow. The course bridges fundamental concepts with engineering practice, culminating in a concrete software implementation: **Double-Entry Ledger & Financial Statement Generator**.

---

## 🔗 Prerequisites
- **Formal Prerequisites:** None (Direct Entry)
- **Recommended Foundations:** See detailed [Prerequisites Document](./overview/prerequisites.md).

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Master the theoretical formulations and mathematical foundations of Accounting Equation & Double-Entry Bookkeeping.
- Implement verifiable algorithms and architectures utilizing Python, SQL, Excel / Sheets.
- Analyze computational trade-offs, complexity limits, and reliability factors.
- Complete and test the practical course engineering project: **Double-Entry Ledger & Financial Statement Generator**.

---

## 📚 Topics
1. **Accounting Equation & Double-Entry Bookkeeping**
2. **Journal Entries, General Ledger & Trial Balance**
3. **Financial Statements: Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow**
4. **Adjusting Entries & Closing the Books**
5. **Inventory Costing Methods (FIFO, LIFO, Weighted Average)**
6. **Internal Controls & Financial Ratios**
