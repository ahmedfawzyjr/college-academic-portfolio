# Executive Overview: HU201 - Principles of Accounting

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Principles of Accounting (مبادئ المحاسبة)
- **Code:** `HU201`
- **Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 2nd Year - Term 2

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Principles of Accounting.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
