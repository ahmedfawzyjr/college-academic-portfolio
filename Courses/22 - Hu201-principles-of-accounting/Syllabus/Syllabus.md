# Course Syllabus: HU201 — Principles of Accounting

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y2S2 (Year 2, Semester 4)  
**Credit Hours:** 2 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Accounting Equation & Double-Entry Bookkeeping
- **Week 2**: Journal Entries, General Ledger & Trial Balance
- **Week 3**: Financial Statements: Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Cash Flow
- **Week 4**: Adjusting Entries & Closing the Books
- **Week 5**: Inventory Costing Methods (FIFO, LIFO, Weighted Average)
- **Week 6**: Internal Controls & Financial Ratios

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
