# Measurable Learning Outcomes: CS491 — Special Topics in Computer Science

By the conclusion of this course, students demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Explain and Formulate**: Articulate the theoretical principles of Cloud Computing Architecture (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS) and Microservices Architecture & RESTful APIs.
2. **Design and Implement**: Build, debug, and document working engineering implementations of Containerized Microservices Architecture & CI/CD Pipeline.
3. **Evaluate and Optimize**: Evaluate algorithmic or system trade-offs using empirical benchmarks and theoretical bounds.
4. **Integrate**: Connect Special Topics in Computer Science to modern software engineering workflows and architectural design patterns.
