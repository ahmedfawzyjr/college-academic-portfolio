# Course Syllabus: CS491 — Special Topics in Computer Science

**Department:** Computer Science Department  
**Academic Term:** Y4S1 (Year 4, Semester 7)  
**Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours  
**Instruction Format:** Lectures (Sessions), Practical Laboratory Exercises, Graded Assignments  

---

## 📅 Weekly Topic Distribution
- **Week 1**: Cloud Computing Architecture (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS)
- **Week 2**: Microservices Architecture & RESTful APIs
- **Week 3**: Containerization with Docker & Container Orchestration
- **Week 4**: Serverless Computing & Cloud Functions
- **Week 5**: DevOps Pipelines & Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment
- **Week 6**: Edge Computing & Emerging Architectural Paradigms

---

## 📊 Assessment & Grading Matrix
- **Midterm Examination:** 20%
- **Practical Laboratory & Quizzes:** 20%
- **Assignments & Course Engineering Project:** 20%
- **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 40%
- **Total:** 100%
