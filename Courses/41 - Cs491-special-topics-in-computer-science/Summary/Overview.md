# Executive Overview: CS491 - Special Topics in Computer Science

## Course Summary
- **Course Name:** Special Topics in Computer Science (موضوعات خاصة في علوم الحاسب)
- **Code:** `CS491`
- **Credit Hours:** 3 Credit Hours
- **Level:** 4th Year - Term 1

## Key Learning Outcomes
1. Master core theoretical principles and foundational concepts of Special Topics in Computer Science.
2. Apply practical algorithms, tools, and methodologies in solving real-world problems.
3. Complete structured lab exercises, code examples, and assignment implementations.
